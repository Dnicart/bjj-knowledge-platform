Purpose

BJJ Knowledge Platform helps beginners understand Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu through clear, organized, and interactive educational content. It is designed as a learning resource, not a replacement for qualified instruction at a certified academy.

Disclaimers

  • This site does not provide medical advice. Consult a healthcare provider before starting training.
  • Belt promotion timelines vary by academy and are not guaranteed.
  • Competition rules are summarized for education — verify with the governing organization.
  • All technique descriptions are original educational content.

Technology

Built with Angular 22, TypeScript, SCSS, and Angular SSR with prerendering.
